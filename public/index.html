Help Center

How do I start a conversation?
Simply type your message in the chat input box and press enter or click the send button.
Can I copy the bot's responses?
Yes, click the copy button below any bot message to copy its content to your clipboard.
How can I edit images using a link?
You can easily edit or transform any image using the !nanoedit command.

Usage:
!nanoedit <image_link> | <prompt>

Example:
!nanoedit https://example.com/photo.jpg | turn this person into a cartoon superhero

➤ The bot will automatically process the image using Nano Banana AI and send back the edited image.

💡 Tip: Make sure your image link is public and ends with .jpg, .png, or .jpeg.
How do I create a post?
To create a post:

1. Click on "Posts Feed" in the menu
2. Click the "Create Post" button
3. Type your message (up to 500 characters)
4. Optionally add photos or videos
5. Click "Post" to share with the community

You can also like and comment on other users' posts!
How do I find and connect with other users?
To find and connect with other users:

1. Click on "All Users" in the menu
2. Browse through the user directory
3. Use the search bar to find specific users
4. Filter users by status (Online, New, Active)
5. Click on any user card to:
• View their profile
• Send them a private message
• See their activity stats

This feature helps you discover and connect with people who share similar interests!
How do I play Checkers?
To play multiplayer checkers:

1. Click on "Checkers" in the menu
2. Create a new room (you'll get a 6‑character code) or join an existing room with a code.
3. Wait for a second player to join (first player is white, second is black).
4. Click on a piece to select it, then click on a highlighted square to move.
5. Capturing is forced – if you can capture, you must capture.
6. Kings are crowned when they reach the opposite side.

The game updates in real‑time for both players.
How do I change my settings?
Navigate to the Settings section through the menu to customize your experience.
Is my data secure?
Yes, we use secure authentication and encryption to protect your conversations and personal data.
