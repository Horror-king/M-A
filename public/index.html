Help Center

How do I start a conversation?
Simply type your message in the chat input box and press enter or click the send button.
Can I copy the bot's responses?
Yes, click the copy button below any bot message to copy its content to your clipboard.
How can I edit images using a link?
Use the !nanoedit command: !nanoedit <image_link> | <prompt>
How do I create a post?
Click "Posts Feed" in the menu, then the "Create Post" button.
How do I find and connect with other users?
Click "All Users" in the menu to browse the user directory.
How do I play Checkers?
Click "Checkers" in the menu, create or join a room, then play.
How do I change my settings?
Navigate to the Settings section through the menu.
Is my data secure?
Yes, we use secure authentication and encryption.
How can I contact support?
Email us at: trex28806@gmail.com
